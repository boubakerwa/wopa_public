UK MTD roadmap

Making Tax Digital, without overclaiming.

WOPA is WhatsApp-first invoicing for UK tradespeople. This page explains what is available now, what is planned, what is still research, and what is not currently available for Making Tax Digital.

Join the waitlist Read VAT guide
Important position

WOPA is not currently MTD-compatible software.

WOPA does not currently appear on HMRC's compatible software lists, does not file VAT returns, and does not submit Making Tax Digital for Income Tax quarterly updates. If you are required to use MTD, use HMRC-recognised compatible software, bridging software, or an accountant-led workflow.

GOV.UK guidance says MTD for Income Tax starts from 6 April 2026 for sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000, then over £30,000 from April 2027 and over £20,000 from April 2028. Always check current HMRC guidance before making compliance decisions.
Roadmap status

What WOPA can and cannot do.

Available now

WhatsApp invoicing records

Create and track invoices from WhatsApp, including VAT, domestic reverse charge VAT wording, CIS deduction support, PDF invoices, email delivery, and payment reminders.

Available now

Practical accountant handoff

WOPA can help keep invoice details together so you are not reconstructing jobs from scattered chats. This is useful operationally, but it is not the same as a complete MTD digital record system.

Planned

Structured exports

CSV-style exports for invoices, customers, VAT amounts, CIS deductions, payment status, and due dates, designed to make handoff to accountants and bookkeeping software cleaner.

Planned

MTD preparation views

Quarterly summaries, missing-information checks, and clearer VAT/CIS reporting views so tradespeople can prepare data before sending it to an accountant or compatible product.

Research

Digital links and integrations

Researching whether WOPA should connect to recognised accounting products, bridging tools, or other digitally linked workflows without forcing users into a heavy dashboard.

Research

Direct MTD software pathway

Researching the product, support, audit, and HMRC API requirements for direct MTD submissions. No launch date is being claimed.

Not currently available

HMRC recognition or MTD filing

WOPA is not currently HMRC-recognised MTD-compatible software. It does not submit VAT returns, Income Tax quarterly updates, End of Period Statements, or Final Declarations.

Not currently available

Full accounting features

WOPA does not currently offer bank feeds, payroll, double-entry bookkeeping, corporation tax workflows, stock accounting, or accountant practice management.
