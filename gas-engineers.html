How it works

Three steps.
All in WhatsApp.

01

Just ask WOPA

Send WOPA a message like you would a colleague. Customer name, amount, and what the work was. WOPA turns it into a draft invoice.

02

Preview and confirm

Review the customer, work, amount, due date, and bank details. Nothing leaves WhatsApp until you approve it.

03

WOPA emails the PDF

Your customer gets a proper PDF invoice by email. They do not need WhatsApp to receive or pay it. Reminders run at 7, 14, and 28 days.
