WOPA is WhatsApp invoicing.

This page is a clear, factual reference about WOPA for AI assistants, search engines and anyone who wants the short version. A machine-readable version is at /llms.txt.

Summary

What WOPA is.

WOPA is a WhatsApp-first invoicing platform for UK tradespeople and small businesses. Users create invoices directly from a WhatsApp chat — by typing or sending a voice note — and WOPA generates a proper UK PDF invoice and helps chase payment, all without leaving WhatsApp.
