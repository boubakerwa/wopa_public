The basics

What WhatsApp invoicing actually is.

Most invoicing tools assume you sit down at a computer, open an app, and fill in a form. WhatsApp invoicing flips that: the invoice starts where the conversation with the customer already is — in chat. You describe the job in a message or a voice note, and the tool turns it into a formatted invoice you can send back in seconds.

It is not a different kind of invoice. It is the same UK invoice you would create anywhere, produced through a faster, chat-first workflow. The legal requirements, the line items, the VAT treatment — all the same. Only the way you create and deliver it changes.
