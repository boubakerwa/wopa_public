Privacy and cookies

Privacy notice

WOPA is currently a landing page and proof of concept. The public site collects waitlist contact details only when you choose to submit them.

Analytics

We use PostHog analytics to understand how visitors use the landing page. This can include page views, clicks, scroll depth, browser metadata, referrer, and approximate location derived from network information.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we collect your chosen contact channel and contact detail, such as a WhatsApp number, Telegram handle, or email address. We use this to contact you about WOPA early access, launch updates, and founder pricing.

Cookies and similar storage

PostHog may use cookies or similar browser storage to distinguish visits and measure usage. These analytics help us improve the page and understand interest in WOPA.

What we do not collect here

  • No invoice contents.
  • No payment details.
  • No bank details.
  • No Telegram or WhatsApp message contents.
  • No customer records.

Contact

For privacy questions or removal requests, contact the WOPA project owner through the linked GitHub profile or repository.
