What's included

Built for the job site.
Runs on the job site.

Natural language, Claude-powered

Say "Invoice Dave at Acme £1,200 for boiler install." WOPA extracts the client, amount, description and due date. Asks for anything missing. Confirms before sending.

LLM extraction Multi-turn Context-aware

Professional PDF invoices

An A4 PDF is generated automatically and emailed to your client with your bank details, sort code, account number, and payment reference. No logo required, but it looks the part.

PDF email Bank details Numbered

Outstanding at a glance

Ask "Who hasn't paid?" and get an instant list sorted by days overdue, with running totals. One message to chase everyone who's late.

Late Payment Act in one word

At 30 days overdue, WOPA prompts you. Say yes and it sends a formal notice citing the Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act 1998, which entitles you to interest plus a fixed fee.

England & Wales Your approval

Stateful conversations, no context ever lost

Say "Invoice Dan £450" on Monday. Come back Tuesday and say "for the plumbing at 42 Albany Road." WOPA remembers. State is stored in Postgres with advisory locks preventing race conditions. Every invoice requires an explicit confirmation before it goes out. You are always in control.
