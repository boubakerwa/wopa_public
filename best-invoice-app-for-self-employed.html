What to look for

Choosing when you are self-employed.

When you are a one-person business, time spent on admin is time not earning. The things that matter are: can you create a valid UK invoice quickly (ideally from your phone), will it chase late payers so you do not have to, and does it keep tidy records for your Self Assessment. WOPA covers all three from WhatsApp.

If you also want bookkeeping, expense tracking and tax estimates, weigh that honestly — an accounting package like FreeAgent does that better, and WOPA can sit in front of it as the fast invoicing layer.
