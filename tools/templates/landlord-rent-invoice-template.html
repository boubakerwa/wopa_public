Landlord checklist

Make the rent period impossible to miss.

For property income, the key is connecting the tenant, property, rent period, due date, and payment record.

Property and tenant

  • Tenant name.
  • Property address.
  • Landlord or managing agent details.
  • Tenancy or account reference.

Rent details

  • Rent period covered.
  • Rent amount due.
  • Service charge or utilities if invoiced separately.
  • Arrears brought forward if relevant.

Payment

  • Due date.
  • Bank details and payment reference.
  • Late payment or arrears note.
  • Contact for queries.

Records

  • Keep copies of invoices and payments.
  • Separate property income and expenses.
  • Capture repairs and letting costs digitally.
  • Check MTD dates if property income is above thresholds.

Example rent invoice wording

RENT INVOICE Landlord: Greenfield Property Ltd Tenant: Jordan Ellis Property: 22 Park View, Sheffield Invoice/reference: RENT-2026-06 Rent period: 1 June 2026 to 30 June 2026 Monthly rent: GBP 950.00 Arrears brought forward: GBP 0.00 Total due: GBP 950.00 Due date: 1 June 2026 Payment reference: 22PARK-JUNE
This is record and payment guidance, not legal tenancy advice. Formal notices require the right legal process.
