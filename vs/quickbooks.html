WOPA vs QuickBooks

WOPA vs QuickBooks
for UK tradespeople.

QuickBooks is full accounting software built for businesses that need VAT filing, bank feeds, and payroll. WOPA is a WhatsApp bot that does one thing: invoicing and payment chasing. If you just need to send invoices and get paid, you probably do not need accounting software.

Why consider WOPA

Less software.
More invoicing.

No app to open

WOPA runs inside WhatsApp. You do not need to log in to a dashboard, download a mobile app, or learn a new interface. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can create an invoice.

Built for tradespeople who just invoice

Many sole traders only need to send invoices and chase payments. QuickBooks is powerful accounting software, but that power comes with complexity most tradespeople do not need.

Human-in-the-loop on firm follow-ups

WOPA sends polite reminders automatically but asks you to review and confirm before any firm follow-up goes out. You stay in control of the tone.

Honest about limitations

WOPA does not support VAT, payment links, or accounting exports. If you need those, QuickBooks is the better tool. WOPA is for tradespeople who want the simplest path from job done to invoice sent.
