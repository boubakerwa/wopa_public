What to look for

Choosing WhatsApp invoicing software.

If invoicing from WhatsApp is the point, the things that matter are: can you create the invoice from a chat message (not just attach one you made elsewhere), does it produce a valid UK invoice as a PDF, and will it help you chase unpaid invoices. WOPA is built around those three.

If you also need bookkeeping, bank feeds, VAT returns or MTD submissions, weigh that honestly — a dedicated accounting package will serve you better there, and you can pair it with a faster invoicing front end.
