The right app depends on how you work. If you invoice stage payments as the job moves, a WhatsApp-first tool keeps it fast. If you need full bookkeeping and CIS accounting, accounting software wins. Here is the honest comparison for UK builders.

What to look for

Choosing as a builder.

On site, juggling subcontractors and stage payments, the friction is stopping to open a separate app to send an invoice. The things that matter for a builder are: can you create the invoice straight from a chat message, does it produce a valid UK invoice (with CIS and VAT handled where relevant), and will it chase clients reliably. WOPA is built around those.

If you also need full CIS accounting, bank feeds or MTD submissions, weigh that honestly — dedicated accounting software does that better, and WOPA can sit in front of it as the fast invoicing layer.
