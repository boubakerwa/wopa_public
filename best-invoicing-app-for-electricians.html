The best invoicing app for electricians, honestly compared.

The right app depends on how you work. If you invoice from the van between jobs, a WhatsApp-first tool wins on speed. If you need full bookkeeping, accounting software wins. Here is the honest comparison for UK electricians.

QuickBooks, Xero and Tradify are genuinely strong tools for accounting and job management. The gap WOPA fills is invoicing that starts in WhatsApp, where electricians already talk to customers.
What to look for

Choosing as an electrician.

On the tools, on a ladder, phone in hand — the friction is opening a separate app to bill a job. The things that matter for an electrician are: can you create the invoice straight from a chat message, does it produce a valid UK invoice (with VAT and CIS handled where relevant), and will it chase the customer when they forget to pay. WOPA is built around those.

If you also need bookkeeping, bank feeds or MTD submissions, weigh that honestly — dedicated accounting software does that better, and WOPA can sit in front of it as the fast invoicing layer.
