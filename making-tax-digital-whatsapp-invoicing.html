The deadline

Making Tax Digital is becoming a buying trigger.

HMRC guidance says MTD for Income Tax starts from 6 April 2026 for some sole traders and landlords, then expands by income threshold in later years. That makes MTD a practical software decision, not just a tax topic.

6 Apr 2026
MTD for Income Tax begins for some sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000.
Apr 2027
The threshold expands to those with qualifying income over £30,000.
Apr 2028
The threshold is planned to reduce to over £20,000.

Check GOV.UK for the latest rules: find out if and when you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.

Product direction

What WOPA can make simpler.

The opportunity is not to replace every accounting platform. It is to make the daily record-creation step effortless for people who already run their business from WhatsApp.

Invoice records

Create consistent invoice records from a short WhatsApp message, with invoice number, customer, dates, line items, payment terms, VAT, and PDF delivery.

MTD preparation

Keep the workflow pointed toward digital records, exportability, and accountant handoff instead of loose screenshots, voice notes, and one-off PDFs.

Accountant handoff

Give accountants cleaner source data: invoices, paid status, VAT treatment, CIS details, and customer context in a format they can review.
