How it works

Three steps.
All in WhatsApp.

01

Message WOPA

Tell WOPA who to invoice, how much, and for what. Use plain English, the way you would text a colleague.

02

Approve the draft

WOPA shows a preview with customer, amount, and due date. Nothing is sent until you reply YES.

03

Invoice sent by email

Your customer receives a professional PDF invoice by email with your bank details. Reminders run automatically at 7, 14, and 28 days.
