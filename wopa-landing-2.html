// what's included
Built for the
Job Site.

Natural Language

Powered by Claude. Say "Invoice Dave at Acme for £1200 for boiler install" and it figures the rest out. Multi-turn collection for missing details.

LLM extraction Slot filling Context-aware

PDF Invoices

Professional A4 PDF generated via Puppeteer and sent as an email attachment. Includes your bank details, sort code, and payment reference.

Puppeteer PDF Email delivery Resend

Outstanding Summary

Ask "Who hasn't paid?" and get a live list sorted by days unpaid, with totals. One-tap mass chase for overdue clients.

Late Payment Act

At 30 days, WOPA prompts you to send a firmer notice referencing statutory interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act 1998.

England & Wales Auto-triggered

Stateful conversations — no context lost

Say "Invoice Dan £450" on Monday. Follow up with "for plumbing" on Tuesday. WOPA tracks pending state per user in Postgres, with advisory locks preventing race conditions. Confirmations required before any invoice is sent or any email goes out — you're always in control.

Postgres state Advisory locks pg-boss jobs Confirmation gates TypeScript
